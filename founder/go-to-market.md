# DesignEase go-to-market plan

Assumption: DesignEase is live at www.designease.io with paid plans, so this is a growth plan, not a launch plan.
Assumption: the plan in founder/pricing-strategy.md is adopted: free designer tier, paid Signature and Firm, supplier fees and a materials commission.
Assumption: the product could not be opened from here (network block). Product notes below come from the public site description, not from using it.

## 1. Readiness check

**The market is small. Size it before spending.**
- Bahrain has about 261 interior designers and 162 interior decorators listed (https://www.poidata.io/report/interior-designer/bahrain, June 2026). Directory counts, so expect overlap and inactive listings.
- Estimate: if 30% of 261 pay 49 BHD a month, that is 78 accounts and about 3,840 BHD MRR. That is the ceiling for designer subscriptions in Bahrain alone.
- So the business is the supplier side and the materials commission. Designers are the audience suppliers pay to reach. Every GTM decision below follows from that.

**Biggest risk:** the chicken-and-egg problem. Designers won't use a library with thin local stock, and suppliers won't pay for a network with few designers. Solve it by signing suppliers first, by hand, for free.

**Second risk:** larger, funded players moving in. BRKZ (Riyadh, building materials procurement) raised $31M in September 2026 and serves 1,500+ contractors and 150 factories (https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/14/3360818/0/en/brkz-secures-31-million-to-scale-ai-enabled-building-materials-procurement-across-saudi-arabia-and-the-gcc.html). It sells to contractors today, not designers, but finishes are a short step. Your defence is the designer workflow (boards, BOQ, renders), which a procurement tool doesn't have.

## 2. Pre-launch of the new model (next 4 weeks)

**Suppliers first (week 1 to 2)**
- List every tile, parquet, stone and quartz showroom in Bahrain. Target: the 20 that designers name most. Ask your current users which 20.
- Visit in person. Offer: free listing for 3 months, you photograph and upload their catalog, they get every BOQ line that names their product.
- Goal: 15 suppliers live, covering all 6 categories, before you announce anything to designers.

**Designers (week 2 to 4)**
- A founding-studio list, not a public waitlist: 50 places at 39 BHD for 12 months. A real limit is the reason to join.
- Book 20 one-to-one demos with Bahrain studios. In a market of 261, every designer is reachable by name.

**Assets**
- A 60-second screen recording: pick 3 tiles for a bathroom board, get the BOQ with wastage, send it to 2 suppliers, compare quotes side by side. Arabic and English captions.
- Social proof: give 5 studios free Signature for 3 months in exchange for a case study with real numbers: [hours saved per BOQ], [quote turnaround in days]. Don't publish numbers until you have them.
- Pricing page copy: run /founder:landing-page.

## 3. Launch: channels that fit

Product Hunt, Hacker News and Reddit don't fit. The buyers are a few hundred Gulf designers who don't gather there.

**Instagram (main channel).** Interior designers in the Gulf sell through Instagram. Post the before/after: mood board, then Vision Lab render, then the actual BOQ total. Tag the supplier whose material is in it; they will repost to their followers for free.

**LinkedIn.** For firm owners and suppliers. One post a week from the founder about what Bahrain designers actually spend time on, with a number from your own data, e.g. [average wastage % added per BOQ]. Post Sunday to Tuesday mornings, Gulf time.

**WhatsApp.** Designers and suppliers already trade quotes on WhatsApp. Make every BOQ and every quote shareable as a WhatsApp link that opens on DesignEase. Each shared BOQ is an ad shown to a supplier or a client.

### Content (5 ideas, search intent)
1. "Porcelain vs ceramic tiles in Bahrain: price per m² in 2026" with your own supplier data. Arabic and English.
2. "How much wastage to add for tiles, parquet and stone" with a free calculator page that leads to the BOQ tool.

Reuse: each article becomes 1 Instagram carousel, 1 LinkedIn post and 1 WhatsApp broadcast to your designer list.

- StartUp Bahrain, run by Tamkeen: pitch events and visibility (https://startupbahrain.com/blog/how-tamkeen-is-championing-entrepreneurship-in-the-kingdom).
- Tamkeen Digital Enablement and AI Program: check whether studios can get a subsidy on DesignEase subscriptions (https://www.gccbusinessnews.com/bahrain-tamkeen-launch-digital-ai-program/). If yes, that removes the price objection.
- Downtown Design Dubai, 4 to 8 November 2026 at d3 (https://ifdm.design/2026/08/06/the-gulf-s-autumn-calendar/): go as a visitor to meet GCC suppliers, not to exhibit.
- CIDEX Dammam, 14 to 17 December 2026: the first step into the Eastern Province (https://www.showsbee.com/events-343-1.html, listing, confirm dates).
- Partnership 1: one large tile or stone supplier as anchor, with its full catalog live, in exchange for 6 months of commission-free sales.
- Partnership 2: a fit-out contractor that receives DesignEase BOQs directly, so the BOQ becomes the document the whole project runs on.

**Growth tactic specific to DesignEase:** put the DesignEase BOQ in the client's hands. When a designer sends the client a branded BOQ with live supplier prices, the client sees "Made with DesignEase" and every supplier on it gets a lead. One BOQ reaches 1 client and 2 to 5 suppliers.

## 6. Budget (up to about 190 BHD, $500 a month)

- Instagram boosts on supplier-tagged posts: 60 BHD
- Fuel, coffee and printed QR cards for showroom visits: 40 BHD
- Arabic translation and captions: 40 BHD
- Tools (analytics, scheduling): 0 to 20 BHD on free tiers

**Best spend under $200:** a professional photographer for one day across 5 anchor showrooms, about [quote in BHD]. Because good material photos are what make the library worth using, and suppliers will reuse them and credit you.

## 7. Ways to improve DesignEase (product)

Ranked by effect on the two-sided loop.

1. **BOQ to quote to order in one flow.** Today quotes are compared, then the deal leaves the platform. Add checkout, so the commission in section 7 of pricing-strategy.md can be earned.
2. **WhatsApp-native sharing.** Share a board, BOQ or quote request as a WhatsApp link; suppliers reply without an account.
3. **Supplier self-serve upload.** CSV or photo upload with AI tagging (size, finish, slip rating, stock). Removes you as the bottleneck after the first 20 suppliers.
4. **Live stock and lead time** per SKU. Designers in the Gulf lose projects to "out of stock, 10 weeks from Italy."
5. **Arabic interface** and right-to-left boards, if not already there. Check against the site.
6. **Client view.** A read-only link where the client approves materials and sees the total. This is where "Made with DesignEase" spreads.
7. **Price index and benchmarks** from your own quote data. Only you can produce this; it is the content engine and a reason for press coverage.
8. **Samples.** Request physical samples from the board, delivered by the supplier. Material Bank built its business on this.
9. **Vision Lab renders that use real SKUs.** A render showing the exact tile, with its price, is worth more than a generic AI image. This is the link between AI and the commission.
10. **Fair-use AI credits** instead of unlimited (see pricing-strategy.md section 4).
